## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineering student at IUT** and an **AI Intern at Advanced AI Labs**, where I build computer-vision systems for **automated fabric quality inspection** in the garment (RMG) industry. Outside the lab I build **full-stack, event-driven products** for an early-stage startup and keep entering hackathons.

- 🧵 **Now:** designing and benchmarking defect-detection pipelines (segmentation, detection and anomaly detection) on high-resolution industrial images
- 🏗️ **Building:** a multi-tenant omnichannel messaging platform: realtime inbox, queues and an admin CRM
- 🌱 **Learning:** model evaluation at scale, GPU training workflows, Kubernetes and distributed tracing
- 🏆 **Hackathons:** speech-to-structured-data, LLM agents and decision tools
- 💬 **Ask me about:** CV pipelines, NestJS architecture, PostgreSQL-first design, observability
